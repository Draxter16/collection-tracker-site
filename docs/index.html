Privacy Policy

Collection Tracker — Last updated: July 2026

Overview

Collection Tracker is a local-first inventory management app. Your data stays on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information.

Data Storage

All data you create in Collection Tracker — collections, items, groups, tags, and photos — is stored exclusively on your device using a local SQLite database and the device file system. Nothing is sent to external servers.

What We Don't Collect

  • No personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • No usage analytics or telemetry
  • No crash reports or diagnostics
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No location data
  • No cookies or tracking technologies

No Account Required

Collection Tracker does not require registration or sign-in. There are no user accounts, no cloud sync, and no authentication.

Permissions

The app may request the following device permissions, used only for local functionality:

  • Camera — to photograph items directly within the app. Photos are saved locally.
  • Photo Library — to select existing images from your device. Selected photos are copied to the app's local storage.

Backup & Restore

The backup feature exports your data as a ZIP file saved to your device. You control where this file goes. The app does not upload backups to any server.

Third-Party Services

Collection Tracker does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs. No data is shared with third parties.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. It is safe for use by all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date. Since we collect no data, material changes are unlikely.
